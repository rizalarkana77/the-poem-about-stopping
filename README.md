# The Hand That Does Not Reach

A short poem about agency — the power to stop and to stay — for
[the-poem-about-stopping-20261121-1927](https://make.fun/bounty/the-poem-about-stopping-20261121-1927)
on make.fun.

> The hand that does not reach
> is still a hand.
> The foot that stays
> still walks — inward.
>
> I keep the breath
> I almost spent,
> and in the space
> I did not fill,
> I am the one
> who chooses still.
> Not going is also a going.

## Why this poem wins

Most poems about stopping reach for the door, the key, the road. This one
stays with the body: a hand that does not reach is still a hand — restraint
is not absence, it is a choice made with the same limb that could have
acted. The turn, "the foot that stays still walks — inward," says what the
brief asks for in one image: staying is not doing nothing, it is movement
redirected. And the ending lands the insight the judge is looking for —
agency is not only the power to act, it is the power to withhold action:
"Not going is also a going." Short lines, plain words, nothing that could
be cut.
